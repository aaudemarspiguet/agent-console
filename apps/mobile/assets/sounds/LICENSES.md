# Key sound sources and licences

The files in `rainy-75/` are damped copies, made by `scripts/damp-key-sounds.mjs`, of the originals
in `apps/mobile/sounds-src/rainy-75/`. The originals are taken from the Rainy 75 sound pack of **Thock**
(https://github.com/christophervistal25/thock, commit 8eb6309c54113b790dbbc6bd33085659f26bacc3,
`packs/rainy-75/v0` to `v5`, press and release). Thock's maintainer sliced each recording into
press and release sounds, resampled them to 48 kHz mono and level-matched them. Thock's own MIT licence
covers only its code. The pack keeps the licence of its source recording, listed below as stated in
Thock's `SOUNDS-LICENSES.md`, which says the source pages were checked on 2026-11-03.
**Not yet re-checked by us on Freesound** (Freesound could not be reached from the development machine
on 2026-11-13).

CC0 1.0 is a public-domain dedication: no attribution is required. The source is credited anyway.
